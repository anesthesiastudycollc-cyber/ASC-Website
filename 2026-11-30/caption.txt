A sustained ETCO2 waveform is the standard for confirming tracheal placement. No CO2 means esophageal intubation until proven otherwise: if in doubt, take it out and oxygenate. The SpO2 can stay normal for minutes after good preoxygenation, so it won't warn you in time. No trace = wrong place.
