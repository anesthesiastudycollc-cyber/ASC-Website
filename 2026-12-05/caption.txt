Burns, denervation and prolonged immobility spread extra acetylcholine receptors across the muscle membrane. Succinylcholine opens every one of them and potassium floods out. Calcium first to stabilize the heart, then shift the potassium. Use a nondepolarizing relaxant for these patients after the first day.
