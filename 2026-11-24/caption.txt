Neostigmine or sugammadex: do you know why one can't reverse a deep block? 💊

Swipe for the essentials: neostigmine boosts acetylcholine and has a ceiling → why it needs some recovery first and glycopyrrolate alongside → how sugammadex encapsulates rocuronium and vecuronium (but not cisatracurium or succinylcholine) → why quantitative TOF still decides when the tube comes out.

💡 Sugammadex can reduce the effect of hormonal contraceptives. Counsel patients per your institution's policy.
