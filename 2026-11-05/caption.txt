Beach-chair shoulder under an interscalene block. BP 72/38, HR 42, out of nowhere. What's happening? 🪑

The Bezold-Jarisch reflex: an underfilled, hyperdynamic ventricle triggers a vagal response. Lower the head, give volume, and treat the bradycardia and hypotension quickly.

💡 The brain sits above the cuff, so cerebral perfusion is lower than the number you see.
