Two hours after a thyroidectomy: swelling neck, new stridor, SpO2 89%. What comes first? 🚨

Comment your pick before the answer 👇

An expanding hematoma compresses the airway and causes laryngeal swelling. Call for help and give oxygen, then open the skin and muscle sutures at the bedside to release the clot, before heading to the OR. Plan for a difficult airway even after decompression.
