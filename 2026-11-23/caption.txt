Second dose of succinylcholine for laryngospasm, and the heart rate drops to 38. Why? 💉

Comment your pick before the answer 👇

Succinylcholine is two acetylcholine molecules joined together, so it doesn't just hit the neuromuscular junction: it stimulates cardiac muscarinic receptors too. Bradycardia is more likely with a repeat dose and in children. Atropine is the fix, and many clinicians have it ready before re-dosing.
