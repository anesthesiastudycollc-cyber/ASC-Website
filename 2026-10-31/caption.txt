HR drops to 48 the moment the abdomen inflates. What's your move? 🎃

Peritoneal stretch can trigger a strong vagal response. Talk to the surgeon: release the pneumoperitoneum, then treat with glycopyrrolate or atropine if it persists. Always consider CO2 embolism if ETCO2 or BP crash suddenly.
