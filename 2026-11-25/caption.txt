Older brains need less anesthetic for the same effect. If the patient is stable and the surgical stimulus allows, lighten the anesthetic and watch the raw EEG recover. Check the blood pressure too: hypoperfusion can suppress the EEG. Burst suppression in older adults is linked to postoperative delirium.
