Strabismus surgery, the surgeon tugs on the medial rectus, and the heart rate falls to 42. What's your first move? 👁️

Comment your pick before the answer 👇

Traction on the extraocular muscles fires the oculocardiac reflex: trigeminal afferents in, vagal efferents out. Say it out loud and ask the surgeon to release traction; the rate usually recovers. If it persists or keeps coming back, give atropine and check your depth and ventilation.
